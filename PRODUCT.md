# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who visit César Domínguez's portfolio to understand his experience and work. Prospective employers and clients are likely audiences, inferred from the existing portfolio content.

## Product Purpose

Present César's professional profile, skills, projects, experience, and contact routes in one interactive portfolio. Success means visitors can recognize his focus and open the section relevant to them without navigating away.

## Positioning

The portfolio organizes five professional sections as a single-page experience navigated through custom One Piece wanted-poster controls.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the current facts and copy in Sobre mí, Habilidades, Proyectos, Experiencia, and Contacto.
- Keep the portfolio on one screen without document scrolling; section content may use its own contained panel when needed.
- Keep the section controls usable by pointer and keyboard, and preserve working email, telephone, GitHub, and project links.
- The site is implemented with Astro and React.

## Brand Commitments

- The portfolio belongs to César Domínguez, a Full Stack and DevOps developer from Valencia, Venezuela.
- The user chose One Piece as the single visual theme and requested Sanji as the initial home character, with a different character paired with each section.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing source content in `src/components/GameMenu.tsx` contains the five portfolio sections and contact destinations.
- Existing One Piece chart art and logo are in `public/backgrounds/onepiece.jpg` and `public/logos/onepiece-official.svg`.

## Product Principles

- Keep portfolio facts accurate and recognizable.
- Make the five sections immediately discoverable.
- Keep the experience focused on one page.
