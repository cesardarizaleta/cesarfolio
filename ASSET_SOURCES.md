# Visual assets

## Current illustrated chapters (September 2026)

`public/art/sanji-eclipse.png`, `ellie-eclipse.png`, `zero-two-eclipse.png`, `horimiya-eclipse.png`, and `zoro-eclipse.png` are original AI-generated fan-art illustrations created with Codex's built-in image generator for this portfolio. The user-supplied Japanese game-menu screenshot guided the composition: dark editorial framing, red eclipse, large right-side character, and open space for live text. It was a visual reference only; its text and interface were not copied. Each illustration used a distinct prompt describing the named character and franchise, with a shared direction of hand-inked anime art, black smoke, vermilion slashes, and no baked-in text or UI. Character and franchise rights remain with their respective owners.

The corresponding `public/art/*-tear.png` illustrations are newly generated, closer character scenes for the clickable torn chapter strips. Their irregular edges, red outline, and call to action are CSS and live text rather than baked into the images. Full prompts are in `ART_PROMPTS.md`.

`public/art/black-ops-iii-eclipse.png` and `black-ops-iii-tear.png` are original AI-generated fan-art for the sixth chapter, based on the seated futuristic soldier of [Activision's Black Ops III](https://www.activision.com/games/call-of-duty/call-of-duty-black-ops-3). They do not reproduce game logos or cover typography. Activision owns the franchise and character imagery.

The portfolio stores its artwork locally so each world loads without third-party image requests. Franchise names and artwork belong to their respective owners.

## Wano character cutouts and module backdrops

The active Wano portraits are new transparent illustrations generated for this portfolio with the built-in image generation tool. The existing character cutouts served as pose and identity references; the Wano costume details were guided by the [official ONE PIECE Wano visual](https://one-piece.com/news/o20200722_11261/index.html). The illustrations are generated interpretations, not official Toei renders. Their prompt set was: Sanji in his yellow-and-white striped kimono and dark obi; Zoro in a green patterned kimono, red sash, and three swords; Luffy in his patched red kimono, yellow obi, and straw hat; Nami in her blue floral Wano outfit and bow; Robin in her O-Robi floral kimono, hair ornaments, and fan. All five PNGs have transparent backgrounds.

Each module now loads its own local Wano scenery image. Sources identify the anime scene references; they do not grant an open license. One Piece artwork remains owned by its respective rights holders.
